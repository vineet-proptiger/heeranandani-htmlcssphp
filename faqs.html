Hiranandani Hinjewadi Township is situated at Hinjewadi Phase 3, Krisala Everland, Pune, Maharashtra. The 105-acre integrated development offers direct access to the Pune IT Corridor, Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, Proposed Ring Road, and Mumbai-Pune Expressway.
The township is developed by Hiranandani Group (founded by Dr. Niranjan Hiranandani) in collaboration with Krisala, known for iconic integrated community planning, premium build quality, and sustainable architecture.
The project offers 2 BHK (825 Sq.Ft.), 3 BHK (1,285 Sq.Ft.), and 4 BHK & Duplexes (1,540 Sq.Ft.) residences with modern layouts, large balconies, ample natural ventilation, and unhindered scenic views.
Key amenities include a 105-acre integrated township, 8-acre signature racecourse & polo club, 40-acre Della Resort, collaboration with TERI for air quality improvement, IGBC Platinum Certification in progress, multi-level clubhouse, swimming pool, sports courts, and 24x7 security.
