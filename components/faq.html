Hiranandani Hinjewadi Township is strategically located at Hinjewadi Phase 3, Krisala Everland, Pune, Maharashtra. It enjoys direct proximity to the IT Corridor, Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, Proposed Ring Road, and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.
The township is developed by Hiranandani Group, founded by Dr. Niranjan Hiranandani, one of India's most respected integrated township pioneers, in joint collaboration with Krisala.
The project offers ultra-luxury 2 BHK (825 Sq.Ft.), 3 BHK (1,285 Sq.Ft.), and 4 BHK & Duplexes (1,540 Sq.Ft.) residences with thoughtfully crafted layouts, generous natural ventilation, and panoramic landscape views.
Highlights include a massive 105-acre integrated master plan, 8-acre signature racecourse & polo club, 40-acre Della Resort, collaboration with TERI for eco-sustainability, IGBC Platinum Certification in progress, luxury clubhouse, swimming pool, sports arena, and 24x7 security.
